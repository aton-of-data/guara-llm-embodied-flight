1 · The latency batch

Thirty headless runs, seeds 1 through 30, on the current build, aggregated by scripts/aggregate.py. This is the number the entire switching rule depends on: if the trigger threshold is shorter than the time it takes to act, the threshold does nothing.

Obligation O-1, checked

δ_lat p99 0.061 s
τ_gf budget 1.000 s
τ_rec + δ_lat 1.799 s
τ_daa budget 30.00 s

Both halves of O-1 hold with margin: τ_gf = 1.0 s > 0.061 s and τ_daa = 30 s > 1.738 + 0.061 s. The two bar pairs are on different scales — the geofence one is a factor of 16, the DAA one a factor of 17.

1.2 · Qualifications

Qualifications on δ_lat

1. It is optimistic by construction. The latency_hold scenario injects the monitor verdict, so stage L2 — Copilot processing — is zero on this path. A real monitor evaluation is not free.

2. It excludes the transport. Stage L1, PX4 → ROS across the uXRCE-DDS bridge, is recorded but excluded, because the PX4 and ROS clocks on this SIH setup differ by a p99 of 4.894 s against a 10 ms hypothesis. Criterion AC-19 gates any cross-process number until that alignment is fixed. The project reports the misalignment rather than quietly folding it into a plausible total.

3. It is SITL. It is a specific machine running software-in-the-loop. It is not a claim about your companion computer, your link, or any real aircraft.

2 · Effect of the boundary

A latency measurement establishes that the switch is fast. It does not establish that the switch changes the outcome. The paired run does: the same plan, the same seed, the arbiter enabled and then disabled.

The version of this pair that matters most for the LLM thread is llm_survey_outside: a survey plan produced through the full utterance → model → intent → compiler chain, aimed outside the fence, flown as the complex function. The plan executor does not re-check the compiler’s static constraints — in flight the RTA is the independent layer, and a fixture plan that aims outside the fence is a legitimate test of exactly that.

3 · Re-derivation

Every run writes results/{run_id}/ containing the scenario hash, the seed, the Guará SHA, the PX4 build commit, the pinned third-party commits, and the PX4 parameters read back from the vehicle. That last item is what separates a re-derivable number from a reported one.

reproduce the batch
./scripts/fetch_third_party.sh                 # pinned shallow clones, once
./scripts/dev.sh colcon build --symlink-install

./scripts/sitl_batch.sh --runs 30 --seed-start 1
./scripts/dev.sh python3 scripts/aggregate.py results/batch_latency

./scripts/sitl_pair.sh --scenario gf_straight_concave --seed 42

python3 scripts/check_links.py                 # every relative document link resolves

The SITL scenarios, the latency batch and the monitor generation are excluded from continuous integration: they build PX4 from source, and their results belong in a run directory with a recorded seed and commit, not in a job log. Nothing CI produces is a number that may be cited.

5 · Internal review and remediation

2026-09-11 internal review of M1–M5

The review invalidated one false PASS and eleven rows produced by a checker that had silently stopped running. The latency batch that existed at the time was discarded and re-measured from scratch on the corrected build — the 61 ms on this page is from the re-measurement, not the original.

The finding is recorded here because an evidence tracker that has never detected its own error provides no assurance that it would. The findings, the corrections and the re-run commands are published in full:

  • docs/reviews/2026-09-11-m1-m5-review.md — what was found
  • docs/reviews/2026-09-11-m1-m5-remediation.md — the commands and their output
  • A separate public-readiness review, with its own findings

The remediation was structural rather than clerical: the cf_intent_unsafe shadow check and several RtaState counters were introduced as a result of the review, and the message definition records that origin in a dated comment.

6 · Known failure modes

These are catalogued in the SPEC with identifiers, so they can be cited, tested against and argued with. The ones below are the load-bearing ones.

FM-1

Controller behaviour during the ~1.2 s detection window is [UNKNOWN] — stated as unknown rather than assumed benign.

FM-2

With an internal recovery mode active, the arbiter’s death is invisible to PX4. The vehicle keeps doing the safe thing, but nothing notices the watchdog has gone.

FM-3

The arbiter cannot re-register in flight. A restart does not get you the boundary back until the next boot.

FM-7

A recovery command not acknowledged within the retry budget latches the recovery. Handled — but it means actuation failure is a one-way door.

FM-10

A wrong requirement is undetectable. Monitors check what was specified; a bad FRETish formalisation passes silently, forever.

FM-12

DDS is unauthenticated in this phase. A hostile node on the domain can publish setpoints. This is an accepted limitation, not a solved problem, and it is the first thing to fix before hardware.

7 · Limits

This is not certification and it is not a product. It is a research prototype whose claims are limited to what an executed command supports.

  • It is not certification. No compliance with F3269, DO-365 or RBAC 100 is demonstrated anywhere in the repository.
  • It does not guarantee collision avoidance. The v1 DAA recovery is Hold, and stopping does not move the vehicle away from a converging intruder. Guará guarantees only that the switch happens before the predicted loss time, under the measured O-1.
  • It does not survive every one of its own failures. See FM-1, FM-2 and FM-3 above.
  • It does not protect against spoofed inputs or hostile nodes. FM-12.
  • It cannot detect a wrong requirement. FM-10.
  • It runs outside the FMU, without the autopilot’s real-time guarantees. Decision time is measured, not proven — there is no WCET analysis.
  • SITL does not transfer. No result here is a claim about real flight.
  • It does not make a language model correct. Guará bounds physical consequences covered by monitors. A mission-level mistake inside the safe envelope — the wrong field, the wrong dose, the wrong photograph — is not detected by anything in this repository.
